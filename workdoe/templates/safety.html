Workdoe keeps early conversations focused on project scope, approximate location, and contractor fit.

Approximate locations

Contractors see the city and a rounded ZIP area before a client approves a bid. Workdoe rejects common email, phone, and street-address patterns, but do not put an exact address in any project text.

Private contact

A private message thread opens after the client approves a contractor's mini bid. Keep access codes, payment details, direct contact details, and sensitive documents out of messages.

Review before approval

Clients can compare the proposed scope, price range, timeline, experience, and availability before choosing who to contact.

Check local requirements

Workdoe does not verify provider credentials. Confirm licenses, permits, insurance, inspections, and safe-work requirements directly with the provider and the appropriate local authority.

Report concerns

Report suspicious projects, profiles, photos, or messages from the related Workdoe page. Workdoe moderators can review content and suspend accounts when needed.
