Trust model

Privacy and moderation are part of the MVP, not a later patch.

Workdoe is designed around compartmentalized records, private media routes, and controlled match approvals.

Location privacy

Jobs collect city, state, and ZIP for the DMV beta. Contractors see approximate pins and never receive an exact address through the lead board.

Photo privacy

Job photos are stored outside the public static folder. The app checks the viewer before serving each file.

Account separation

Clients, contractors, and admins have separate role-based dashboards. Password hashes stay in auth records, separate from jobs and media metadata.

Cloudflare path

When this moves to Cloudflare, D1 stores structured records, R2 stores photos, Email Service handles transactional email, and Turnstile protects forms.
